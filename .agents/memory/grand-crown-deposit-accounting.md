---
name: Grand Crown deposit accounting
description: Business rule for member mobile-money deposits and wallet separation.
---

Grand Crown deposits are non-withdrawable and may only be used to purchase products. Credit deposits to the member’s product-fund balance automatically only after payment settlement is confirmed by PesaJet/system. Settlement does not activate a product. Keep withdrawable earnings separate and unchanged.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected the accounting rule.

**How to apply:** Preserve this separation in payment settlement, balance displays, withdrawals, product purchases, admin reports, and member-facing copy.
