---
name: Grand Crown deposit accounting
description: Business rule for member mobile-money deposits and wallet separation.
---

Grand Crown deposits are non-withdrawable and may only be used to purchase products. Members submit mobile-money transfer references, but a reference alone is not proof of payment; an administrator must verify the transfer before approving and crediting the member’s product-fund balance. Approval does not activate a product. Keep withdrawable earnings separate and unchanged.

**Why:** The user changed deposits from provider-settled payments to manual MTN/Airtel transfers with administrator verification.

**How to apply:** Preserve this separation in payment settlement, balance displays, withdrawals, product purchases, admin reports, and member-facing copy.
